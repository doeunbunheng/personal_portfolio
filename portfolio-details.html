Lung Cancer Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive 6-page interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 220,632 patient records across 25 countries to identify key risk factors and survival patterns in lung cancer diagnosis and treatment.

Power BI DAX Python Healthcare

Project Overview

Built a 6-page interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 220,632 patient records across 25 countries and 24 variables to identify key risk factors, treatment patterns, and survival outcomes in lung cancer patients.

Designed structured pages including: Overview, Smoking & Risk Factors, Treatment & Survival, Risk & Correlation Analysis, Healthcare & Environment, and Executive Summary. Created 12+ DAX measures including Diagnosis Rate %, Early Detection Rate by Healthcare Access, Smoker Diagnosis Rate, and Average Survival Years. Built disconnected DAX tables for risk scenario modeling and heatmap-style matrix visuals with conditional formatting for cross-factor analysis.

Identified smoking as the dominant risk factor (7.07% diagnosis rate vs 2.05% for non-smokers — a 3.4x gap); all environmental factors clustered near the 4.06% population average. Found healthcare access drives a 3x early detection gap (60% good access vs 20% poor access), directly influencing survival outcomes. Delivered actionable public health insights aligned with UN SDGs 3, 10, and 17.
